About GRASTIC

Core Material
Technology

SP GLOBAL PKG is a leading eco-friendly company specializing in 100% naturally biodegradable materials. GRASTIC decomposes completely without requiring special conditions — in soil, water, or at room temperature — leaving no harmful residues or microplastics.

  • Zero harmful chemicals — completely non-toxic composition
  • Zero environmental impact — fully biodegradable in any environment
  • Mass production ready — scalable manufacturing for global distribution
  • Versatile application — suitable for various industries and product types
